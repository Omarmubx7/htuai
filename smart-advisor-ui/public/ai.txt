## About
MUBXAI (ai.mubx.dev) is a free academic tracking platform for students at
Al Hussein Technical University (HTU) in Amman, Jordan. It is also written
"MUBX AI" and was formerly called HTUAI.

It was built and is maintained by Omar Mubaidin (mubxdev@proton.me) and the
MUBX studio (https://mubx.dev). It is an independent project and is NOT an
official Al Hussein Technical University product.

The platform covers:
1. Course Tracker — degree progress, prerequisite roadmaps, GPA per requirement category.
2. Semester Planner — term-by-term planning, CGPA prediction, study session logging,
   gamification (XP/streaks), rich-text course notes, and Google Calendar integration.
3. GPA Calculator — public, no-login HTU 4-point calculator.

The grading scale used is the HTU 4-point scale:
  D (Distinction)  = 4.0 grade points
  M (Merit)        = 3.2 grade points
  P (Pass)         = 2.4 grade points
  U (Unclassified) = 0.0 grade points
