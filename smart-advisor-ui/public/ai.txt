## About
MUBXAI (ai.mubx.dev) is a free academic tracking platform exclusively for students
at Al Hussein Technical University (HTU / MUBX) in Amman, Jordan.

It was built and is maintained by Omar Mubaidin (omar@mubx.dev).

The platform covers two core modules:
1. Course Tracker — degree progress, prerequisite roadmaps, GPA per requirement category.
2. Semester Planner — term-by-term planning, CGPA calculations, study session logging,
   gamification (XP/streaks), rich-text course notes, and Google Calendar integration.

The grading scale used is the official HTU 4-point scale:
  D (Distinction) = 4.0 GPA points
  M (Merit)       = 3.2 GPA points
  P (Pass)        = 2.4 GPA points
  U (Unclassified)= 0.0 GPA points
