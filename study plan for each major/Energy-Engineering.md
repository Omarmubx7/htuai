# Bachelor's Degree in Energy Engineering
**Al Hussein Technical University (HTU) — School of Engineering Technology, Energy Engineering Department**
**Study Plan: 2022**

---

## Program Objectives

1. Provide world-class, flexible, STEM-based education which combines in-depth technical knowledge with professional, leadership, and entrepreneurial skills.
2. Build advanced practical and technical skills to enable the student to conceive, design, build, and test complex engineering systems.
3. Provide the student with a hands-on understanding of the evolving market needs through a tailored and intensive industry-based apprenticeship scheme.
4. Create opportunities to enhance the student's character, soft skills, and adaptation abilities within different multidisciplinary teams and changing professional environments.
5. Emphasize the need for engaging in life-long and independent learning and professional development.
6. Equip the student with the up-to-date and relevant preparation for employment in a variety of technical and engineering fields, or for initiating an innovative entrepreneurial venture or a technology startup.
7. Enable the student to progress in graduate studies in relevant technical or engineering fields.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon graduation, the graduate of the Energy Engineering Program should be able to:

1. Apply knowledge of science, mathematics, technology, and engineering fundamentals to energy engineering applications.
2. Design and conduct experiments, as well as analyze and present results in a professional manner.
3. Design, model, analyze and build practical energy systems to meet specific requirements and realistic constraints.
4. Use the techniques, skills, and modern engineering and computing tools necessary for engineering practice.
5. Identify, formulate, and solve engineering problems.
6. Communicate effectively, and function in multidisciplinary teams.
7. Understand professional and ethical issues and the responsibilities of the engineering practice.
8. Recognize contemporary issues and environmental, cultural, and economical consideration of the engineering profession.
9. Appreciate the need for professional development and engage in life-long learning.
10. Engage in entrepreneurial activity and understand the value of technology innovation.
11. Understand the requirements and constraints of the professional employment environment.

---

## Assessment of Learning Outcomes

Assessment tools are accessible, appropriately challenging, and support student self-efficacy and confidence. A robust quality assurance system ensures assignments are valid and reliable. Feedback is both individual (per student) and holistic (per cohort), supporting improvement and recognizing student strengths.

## Key Course Descriptions

### Program Compulsory Courses

**Fundamentals of Thermodynamics and Heat Engines** — Energy forms, properties of pure substances, first/second law of thermodynamics, heat transfer principles, heat engine efficiency. Prerequisite: 00102111.

**Fluid Mechanics** — Pressure and force, fluid flow theory, continuity equation, energy equation, aerodynamics, hydraulic machinery, Newtonian/non-Newtonian fluids, pumps and turbines. Prerequisite: 00102111.

**Electrical and Electronic Principles** — Circuit laws (Kirchhoff, Thevenin, superposition), AC/DC circuits, semiconductor devices (diodes, BJT, FET, MOSFET), analogue vs digital electronics. Prerequisite: 00102111.

**Electronic Circuits and Devices** — Amplifiers, operational amplifiers, oscillators, advanced semiconductor devices, power supply design. Prerequisite: 00103230.

**Heat and Mass Transfer** — Conduction, forced/free convection, heat exchangers, radiation, mass transfer (Fick's law, diffusion, permeability). Prerequisite: 00101240.

**Instrumentation and Control Systems** — Sensing elements (displacement, speed, pressure, temperature, strain), signal conditioning, PID controllers, open/closed loop systems, practical simulations. Prerequisite: 00103230.

**Further Mathematics** — Complex numbers, matrix theory, linear equations, numerical integration, 1st and 2nd order differential equations. Prerequisite: 00103110.

**Professional Engineering Management** — Engineering strategy, TQM, sustainability, managing people, professional standards. Prerequisite: 00102220.

**Lean Manufacturing** — Toyota Production System, lean principles, value stream mapping, waste reduction, process improvement tools. Prerequisite: 00101112.

**Further Thermodynamics** — Irreversibility, heat pumps, refrigeration, air compressors, Rankine cycle (steam plants), Brayton cycle (gas turbines). Prerequisite: 00101240.

**Industrial Power, Electronics and Storage** — Energy demand/production history, efficiency policies, renewable energy interfacing, energy auditing, buildings, electric vehicles, smart grid principles. Prerequisite: 00103240.

**Further Electrical, Electronic and Digital Principles** — Advanced circuit analysis, digital systems, signal fundamentals. Prerequisites: 00103230, 00103213.

**Energy Resources and Conversion** — Overview of conventional and renewable energy sources, conversion technologies, environmental impact, energy auditing. Prerequisite: 00101240.

**Renewable Energy Systems and Technologies** — Solar PV, wind, biomass, geothermal, ocean energy — principles, technologies, and grid integration. Prerequisite: 00102111.

**Power Systems** — Generation, transmission, distribution, load flow, fault analysis, power system protection. Prerequisite: 00103232.

**Energy Efficiency and Management** — Energy auditing methodologies, efficiency measures, demand-side management, energy performance indicators. Prerequisite: 00102330.

**HVAC** — Psychrometrics, heating/cooling load calculation, duct design, refrigeration cycles, ventilation systems. Prerequisite: 00101240.

**Energy Economics, Policy, and Regulations** — Energy pricing, market structures, regulatory frameworks, policy analysis, investment decision-making. Prerequisite: 00102330.

**Photovoltaic Systems** — Solar cell physics, PV system sizing/design, inverters, battery storage, grid-connected and off-grid systems. Prerequisites: 00103232, 00102340.

**Health, Safety, and Environment** — Risk assessment, work equipment safety, fire safety, hazardous substances, environmental awareness and pollution assessment. Prerequisite: 00102321.

**Research Project for Energy Engineers** — Research problem identification, project proposal, literature review, selection of methodology, data analysis, and report writing. Prerequisite: Dept. Approval.

**Capstone Project I & II** — Applied energy engineering project: design, prototyping, testing, documentation, formal design reviews, oral defense. Prerequisite: 00102490.

### Program Elective Courses

**Maintenance Engineering** — Reliability, maintainability, availability, safety, FTA, FMEA, reliability block diagrams. Prerequisite: 00102321.

**Solar Thermal Systems** — Solar collectors (flat plate, evacuated tube), thermal storage, solar water heating, industrial solar heat. Prerequisite: 00101242.

**Wind Energy** — Wind resource assessment, turbine types, aerodynamics, electrical integration, offshore wind. Prerequisite: 00103333.

**Green Buildings** — Building energy standards, passive design strategies, green certification systems (LEED, BREEAM), energy simulation. Prerequisite: 00102111.

**Sustainability** — Natural cycles, UN goals, climate change management, pollution, waste, corporate sustainability, ISO 14001. Prerequisite: 00102340.

**Energy Storage** — Battery technologies, pumped hydro, compressed air, thermal storage, hydrogen, applications. Prerequisite: 00103331.

**Smart Grids** — Smart grid architecture, advanced metering, demand response, grid automation, cybersecurity in energy systems. Prerequisites: 00103331, 00103534.

**Electric and Hybrid Vehicles** — EV/HEV drivetrain architectures, battery management, charging infrastructure, regenerative braking. Prerequisite: 00103333.

**Energy Simulation in Buildings** — Building energy modeling software, simulation-based design, energy performance optimization. Prerequisite: 00101545.

**Electrical Machines** — Electromagnetism principles, transformers, AC motors (induction, synchronous), speed-torque characteristics. Prerequisite: 00103232.
