# Bachelor's Degree in Electrical Engineering
**Al Hussein Technical University (HTU) — School of Engineering Technology, Electrical Engineering Department**
**Study Plan: 2022**

---

## Program Objectives

1. Provide world-class, flexible, STEM-based education which combines in-depth technical knowledge with professional, leadership, and entrepreneurial skills.
2. Build advanced practical and technical skills to enable the student to conceive, design, build, and test complex engineering systems.
3. Provide the student with a hands-on understanding of the evolving market needs through a tailored and intensive industry-based apprenticeship scheme.
4. Create opportunities to enhance the student's character, soft skills and adaptation abilities within different multidisciplinary teams and various professional environments.
5. Emphasize the necessity of engaging in life-long independent learning and professional development.
6. Equip the student with the relevant up-to-date skills for employment in a variety of technical and engineering fields, or initiating an innovative entrepreneurial venture or a technology startup.
7. Enable the student to progress in graduate studies in relevant technical or engineering fields.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon graduation, the graduate of the Electrical Engineering Program should be able to:

1. Apply the knowledge of science, mathematics, technology, and engineering fundamentals to electrical engineering applications.
2. Design and conduct experiments, as well as analyze and present results in a professional manner.
3. Design, model, analyze and build practical electrical systems to meet specific requirements and realistic constraints.
4. Use the techniques, skills and modern engineering and computing tools necessary for engineering practices.
5. Identify, formulate and solve engineering problems.
6. Communicate effectively and function in multidisciplinary teams.
7. Understand professional and ethical issues and the responsibilities of the engineering practice.
8. Recognize contemporary issues and environmental, cultural, and economical considerations of the engineering profession.
9. Appreciate the need for professional development and engage in life-long learning.
10. Engage in entrepreneurial activity and understand the value of technology innovation.
11. Understand the requirements and constraints of the professional employment environment.

---

## Assessment of Learning Outcomes

Assessment model requires students to engage with a variety of tools including design and practical projects, laboratory activities and simulations, take-home assignments, and conventional in-class examinations. A robust quality assurance system using systematic internal-verification procedures monitors assignments in terms of content and grading. Feedback is provided both formatively (throughout the course) and summatively.

## Key Course Descriptions

### School Requirements

**Workshop I** — Hand filing, turning, welding, piping, plumbing, carpentry, brick laying, sheet metal fabrication, and simple computer networks.

**Workshop II** — Manual/electric tools, electric arc welding, spot welding, resistance welding, sand casting, sheet-metal forming, lathing, and metal milling.

**Engineering Math** — Mathematical methods and statistical techniques: progressions, mean/std dev, linear regression, distributions, sine waves, trigonometry, vector functions, differential and integral calculus.

### Program Compulsory Courses

**Electrical and Electronic Principles** — Circuit laws (Kirchhoff, Thevenin, superposition), AC/DC circuits, semiconductor devices (diodes, BJT, FET, MOSFET), analogue vs digital electronics.

**Digital Design** — Number systems, Boolean algebra, combinational and sequential logic circuits, digital systems design.

**Electronic Circuits and Devices** — Amplifiers, operational amplifiers, oscillators, power supplies, and advanced semiconductor devices.

**Instrumentation and Control Systems** — Sensing elements (displacement, speed, pressure, temperature, strain), signal processing, PID controllers, open/closed loop systems, practical experiments.

**Further Mathematics** — Complex numbers, matrix theory, linear equations, numerical integration, differential equations (1st and 2nd order).

**Programmable Logic Controllers** — PLC hardware, ladder logic programming, function blocks, HMI integration, industrial automation.

**Professional Engineering Management** — Engineering strategy, TQM, sustainability, managing people, becoming a professional engineer.

**Industrial Power, Electronics and Storage** — Energy demand/production, efficiency, renewable energy interfacing, energy auditing, smart grids, electric vehicles.

**Industrial Systems** — Industrial automation, SCADA, process control, manufacturing systems, Industry 4.0 technologies.

**Further Electrical, Electronic and Digital Principles** — Advanced circuit analysis, digital systems, signal processing fundamentals.

**Further Control Systems** — Advanced control theory, state-space methods, digital control, system stability analysis.

**Engineering Electromagnetics** — Maxwell's equations, wave propagation, transmission lines, antennas, electromagnetic compatibility.

**Telecommunication Principles** — Modulation/demodulation, digital communications, signal processing, network protocols.

**Electrical Machines** — Electromagnetism, transformers, AC motors (induction, synchronous), speed-torque characteristics.

**Power Systems** — Power generation, transmission, distribution, load flow analysis, fault analysis, protection.

**Embedded Systems** — Microcontroller architecture, real-time programming, interfacing, IoT applications.

**Photovoltaic Systems** — Solar cell fundamentals, PV system design, inverters, grid connection, energy storage.

**Advanced Electronics** — RF circuits, power amplifiers, analog IC design, mixed-signal systems.

**Speech and Image Processing** — DSP fundamentals, speech coding, image processing algorithms, machine learning applications.

**Industry 4.0** — Smart factories, IoT, cyber-physical systems, industrial digitalization, Industry 5.0 trends.

**Health, Safety, and Environment** — Risk assessment, fire safety, electrical safety, environmental awareness, international certification preparation.

**Research Project for Electrical Engineers** — Research problem identification, proposal, literature review, methodology, experimentation, and reporting.

**Capstone Project I & II** — Project design, prototyping, testing, documentation, formal design review, and oral defense.
