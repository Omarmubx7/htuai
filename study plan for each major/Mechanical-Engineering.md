# Bachelor's Degree in Mechanical Engineering
**Al Hussein Technical University (HTU) — School of Engineering Technology**
**Study Plan: 2022 (Version Date: Jan. 2025)**

---

## Program Objectives

- Provide world-class, flexible, STEM-based education which combines in-depth technical knowledge with professional, leadership, and entrepreneurial skills.
- Build advanced practical and technical skills to enable the student to conceive, design, build, and test complex engineering systems.
- Provide the student with a hands-on understanding of the evolving market needs through a tailored and intensive industry-based apprenticeship scheme.
- Create opportunities to enhance the student's character, soft skills, and adaptation abilities within different multidisciplinary teams and changing professional environments.
- Emphasize the need for engaging in life-long and independent learning and professional development.
- Equip the student with the up-to-date and relevant preparation for employment in a variety of technical and engineering fields, or for initiating an innovative entrepreneurial venture or a technology startup.
- Enable the student to progress in graduate studies in relevant technical or engineering fields.

---

## Program Outcomes

Upon graduation, the graduate of the Mechanical Engineering Program should be able to:

1. Apply knowledge of science, mathematics, technology, and engineering fundamentals to mechanical engineering applications.
2. Design and conduct experiments, as well as analyze and present results in a professional manner.
3. Design, model, analyze and build practical mechanical engineering systems to meet specific requirements and realistic constraints.
4. Use the techniques, skills, and modern engineering and computing tools necessary for engineering practice.
5. Identify, formulate, and solve engineering problems.
6. Communicate effectively, and function in multidisciplinary teams.
7. Understand professional and ethical issues and the responsibilities of the engineering practice.
8. Recognize contemporary issues and environmental, cultural, and economical consideration of the engineering profession.
9. Appreciate the need for professional development and engage in life-long learning.
10. Engage in entrepreneurial activity and understand the value of technology innovation.
11. Understand the requirements and constraints of the professional employment environment.

## Key Course Descriptions

### School Requirements

**Functional Math** — Reviews fundamental concepts in numerical analysis, linear algebra, geometry, statistics, and probabilities. Develops mathematical reasoning and problem-formulation skills.

**Functional Physics** — Covers physics topics: measurements, motion, vectors, Newton's laws, energy, static equilibrium, electricity and magnetism.

**Fundamentals of Computing** — In-depth exposure to hardware, operating systems, and C programming including variables, operations, functions, structures, and pointers.

**Introduction to Engineering Drawing** — Fundamentals of engineering graphics: orthographic, isometric, auxiliary projections, sectioning, dimensioning, and CAD software.

**STEM Lab I** — Basic practical skills in mechanical, electrical, electronics, automation, robotics, computer applications, and process control.

**STEM Lab II** — Hands-on with electromechanical systems, robotics, pneumatics, automation, image processing, sensor calibration, and material manufacturing.

**Workshop I** — Hand filing, turning, welding, piping, carpentry, brick laying, surveying, sheet metal fabrication, and simple computer networks.

**Workshop II** — Manual/electric tools, electric arc welding, spot welding, resistance welding, sand casting, sheet-metal forming, lathing, and metal milling.

**Engineering Math** — Mathematical principles for engineering: progressions, statistics, linear regression, distributions, trigonometry, vector functions, differential and integral calculus.

**Engineering Science** — Physical sciences in engineering: units, static/dynamic forces, fluid mechanics, thermodynamics, material properties, A.C./D.C. circuit theories.

**Engineering Design** — Design process: Gantt charts, stakeholder requirements, market analysis, modelling, manufacturability, reliability, safety, ergonomics.

**Managing a Professional Engineering Project** — Project management: planning, stages, Gantt charts, evaluation, communication, and report writing.

**Introduction to AI and Data Sciences** — AI fundamentals: search, machine learning, NLP, analytics, implemented via Python programming.

### Program Compulsory Courses

**Mechanical Principles** — Stress and strain, material properties, bending moments, shear force diagrams, torsion, columns, and structural analysis.

**Fundamentals of Thermodynamics and Heat Engines** — Energy forms, properties of substances, first/second law of thermodynamics, heat transfer, heat engines.

**Fluid Mechanics** — Pressure, submerged surfaces, fluid flow, continuity equation, energy equation, aerodynamics, hydraulic machinery, pipes and pumps.

**Materials, Properties and Testing** — Material structure, properties, testing methods, failure analysis, and selection for engineering applications.

**Further Thermodynamics** — Irreversibility, heat pumps, refrigeration, air compressors, Rankine cycle (steam plants), Brayton cycle (gas turbines).

**Advanced Mechanical Principles** — Advanced stress analysis, fatigue, fracture mechanics, creep, and dynamic loading.

**Further Mathematics** — Complex numbers, matrix theory, linear equations, numerical integration, first and second order differential equations.

**Virtual Engineering** — CAD/CAE tools, finite element analysis, simulation, and virtual prototyping.

**Advanced Manufacturing Technology** — CNC machining, advanced processes, metrology, and quality in manufacturing.

**Professional Engineering Management** — Engineering strategy, TQM, sustainability, managing people, professional standards.

**Machine Design** — Design of machine elements: shafts, gears, bearings, fasteners, and power transmission systems.

**Modelling and Simulation for Engineers** — Mathematical modelling, simulation tools, system identification, and validation.

**Manufacturing Processes** — Casting, forming, machining, joining, and material removal processes.

**HVAC** — Psychrometrics, heating/cooling load calculation, duct design, refrigeration cycles, ventilation systems.

**Heat and Mass Transfer** — Conduction, convection, heat exchangers, radiation, Fick's law, diffusion, and permeability.

**Instrumentation and Control Systems** — Sensing elements, signal processing, PID controllers, open/closed loop systems.

**Dynamics and Vibrations** — Kinematics, kinetics, free/forced vibrations, resonance, and damping.

**Electrical and Electronic Principles** — Circuit laws (Kirchhoff, Thevenin, superposition), semiconductor devices, analogue and digital electronics.

**Advanced Workshop for Mechanical Engineering** — Advanced welding (MIG, TIG, Oxyacetylene), CNC machining, internal combustion engine assembly.

**Health, Safety, and Environment** — Risk assessment, work equipment safety, fire safety, hazardous substances, environmental awareness.

**Research Project for Mechanical Engineering** — Research problem identification, proposal, literature review, methodology, and reporting.

**Capstone Project I & II** — Project design, prototyping, testing, documentation, formal review, and oral defense.

### Program Elective Courses

**Pneumatics and Hydraulic Systems** — Pneumatic and hydraulic components, characteristics, system design, actuators.

**Digital and Additive Manufacturing** — 3D modeling, extrusion, sintering, directed-energy deposition, DFAM, post-processing.

**Advanced Materials** — Shape memory alloys, self-healing materials, piezoelectrics, smart polymers, superalloys.

**Mechatronics** — Sensors, actuators, PLCs, microcontrollers, PID controllers, system design and troubleshooting.

**Maintenance Engineering** — Reliability, maintainability, FTA, FMEA, reliability block diagrams, case studies.

**Composite Materials** — Structure, properties, processing, and applications of composite materials.

**Robotic Systems in Engineering** — Robot kinematics, dynamics, programming, and applications in engineering.

**Renewable Energy Systems and Technologies** — Solar, wind, biomass, and other renewable energy technologies and grid integration.

**Solar Thermal Systems** — Solar collectors, thermal storage, solar water heating, and industrial process heat.

**Advanced Heat and Mass Transfer** — Extended topics in conduction, convection, boiling, condensation, and mass transfer.

**Spacecraft Systems and Design** — Orbital mechanics, spacecraft subsystems, mission design, launch vehicles.

**Mobile Robotics** — Autonomous navigation, localization, mapping, path planning, and robot sensors.

**Industrial Robotics** — Industrial robot types, programming, end-effectors, safety, and manufacturing integration.

**Programmable Logic Controller** — PLC hardware, ladder logic, function blocks, HMI, and industrial automation.
