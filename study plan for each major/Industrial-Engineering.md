# Bachelor's Degree in Industrial Engineering
**Al Hussein Technical University (HTU) — School of Engineering Technology**
**Study Plan: 2023**

---

## Program General Objectives

1. Provide world-class, flexible, STEM-based education which combines in-depth technical knowledge with professional, leadership, and entrepreneurial skills.
2. Prepare graduates to define, analyze, and solve complex problems within and between enterprises.
3. Build advanced practical and technical skills to enable the student to conceive, design, build, and test complex engineering systems.
4. Enable students to discover, understand, and incorporate appropriate new technologies in the design and operation of enterprises.
5. Provide the student with a hands-on understanding of the evolving market needs through a tailored and intensive industry-based apprenticeship scheme.
6. Create opportunities to enhance the student's character, soft skills, and adaptation abilities within different multidisciplinary teams and changing professional environments.
7. Emphasize the need for engaging in life-long and independent learning and professional development.

---

## Program Specific Objectives

1. Develop technical knowledge and skills in core industrial engineering fields, including operations research, production planning, quality control, and manufacturing.
2. Develop technical knowledge and skills in Information Technology and Automation fields, including data analytics, artificial intelligence, enterprise resource planning, and Industry 4.0.
3. Prepare graduates for a range of career paths, including operations management, engineering consultancy, business analysis and transformation, quality assurance, and logistics/supply chain.
4. Enhance critical thinking, decision strategy, and problem-solving skills to solve complex industrial engineering problems.
5. Promote professional management and effective communication skills.

---

## Program Learning Outcomes

1. Apply technical knowledge, scientific principles, and mathematical skills to solve complex engineering problems in various fields, including logistics, operations, and production.
2. Use computer-aided tools to model, simulate, and optimize business operations and industrial systems.
3. Analyze data to make strategic decisions using statistical methods, data analytics tools, quality engineering techniques, and operations research methodologies.
4. Analyze and modify industrial systems using breakthrough technologies, such as Industry 4.0 and artificial intelligence.
5. Lead teams to develop and implement practical solutions to business operations and industrial engineering systems that consider social, environmental, and economic factors.
6. Communicate effectively in written, oral, and graphical forms with diverse audiences, including technical and non-technical stakeholders.

## Key Course Descriptions

### School Requirements

**Functional Math** — Numerical analysis, linear algebra, geometry, statistics, probabilities. Develops mathematical reasoning and problem-formulation skills.

**Functional Physics** — Physics and measurements, motion, vectors, Newton's laws, energy, static equilibrium, electricity and magnetism.

**Fundamentals of Computing** — Hardware, operating systems, C programming (variables, operations, functions, structures, pointers).

**Introduction to Engineering Drawing** — Orthographic, isometric, auxiliary projections, sectioning, dimensioning, CAD software.

**STEM Lab I** — Basic practical skills: mechanical, electrical, electronics, automation, robotics, computer applications, process control.

**STEM Lab II** — Electromechanical systems, robotics, pneumatics, automation, image processing, sensor calibration, material manufacturing.

**Workshop I** — Hand filing, turning, welding, piping, carpentry, brick laying, sheet metal fabrication, simple computer networks.

**Workshop II** — Manual/electric tools, arc welding, spot welding, sand casting, sheet-metal forming, lathing, milling.

**Engineering Math** — Progressions, mean/std dev, linear regression, distributions, sine waves, trigonometry, vector functions, calculus.

**Engineering Science** — Physical sciences: units, static/dynamic forces, fluid mechanics, thermodynamics, materials, A.C./D.C. circuit theories.

**Engineering Design** — Design process: Gantt charts, stakeholder requirements, market analysis, modelling, manufacturability, reliability, safety, ergonomics.

**Managing a Professional Engineering Project** — Project management techniques: planning, stages, Gantt charts, evaluation, reporting.

**Introduction to AI and Data Science** — AI algorithms, machine learning, NLP, data analytics, implemented via Python.

### IE Compulsory Courses

**Further Mathematics** — Number theory, complex numbers, matrix theory, linear equations, numerical integration, 1st and 2nd order differential equations.

**Statistical Process Control (SPC)** — Control charts, process capability assessment, attributes and variables inspection, TQM and Six Sigma applications.

**Data Analytics** — Data analytic terminologies, types of analytics, data exploration, visualization, descriptive/predictive/prescriptive analytics.

**Business Process Support** — Data acquisition, processing into business information, data science techniques applied to real-world business problems.

**Operations Research** — Quantitative methods for decision-making, linear programming, optimization models, simulations.

**Electrical and Electronics Principles** — Circuit laws, Kirchhoff/Thevenin laws, semiconductor devices, analogue vs digital electronics.

**Instrumentation and Control** — Sensing elements, signal conditioning, PID controllers, open/closed loop systems, practical experiments.

**Industry 4.0** — Smart factories, industrial revolutions, smart factory technologies, transition to Industry 4.0, and Industry 5.0 trends.

**Financial Analysis** — Financial statements, ratio analysis, cost-volume-profit, capital budgeting, financial decision-making.

**Industrial Cost Analysis** — Cost classification, cost behavior, standard costing, variance analysis, cost reduction strategies.

**Supply Chain Management** — Supply chain design, procurement, demand planning, supplier relationships, performance metrics.

**Logistics** — Transportation management, warehousing, distribution networks, last-mile delivery, logistics analytics.

**Facility and Asset Management** — Facility planning, layout optimization, asset lifecycle management, maintenance strategies.

**Decision Analysis and Strategy** — Decision trees, multi-criteria analysis, game theory, strategic planning frameworks.

**ERP Systems** — Enterprise resource planning concepts, SAP/Oracle fundamentals, business process integration, implementation.

**Professional Engineering Management** — Engineering strategy, TQM, sustainability, ISO standards, managing engineering teams.

**Sustainability** — Natural cycles, UN millennium goals, climate change, energy-water-food nexus, ISO 14001, ISO 26000.

**Lean Manufacturing** — Toyota Production System (TPS), lean principles, waste reduction, value stream mapping, Kaizen.

**Advanced Manufacturing Technology** — CNC machining, modern manufacturing processes, metrology, quality control.

**Manufacturing Systems Engineering** — Production systems design, scheduling, simulation, automation, Industry 4.0 integration.

**Quality and Process Improvement** — Quality management systems, process improvement methodologies, Six Sigma tools, corrective action.

**Research Project for IE** — Research problem identification, proposal, literature review, methodology, data collection, analysis, and presentation.

**Capstone Project I & II** — Applied industrial engineering project: problem definition, data analysis, solution design, implementation, and evaluation.
